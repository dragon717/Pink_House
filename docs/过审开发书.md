## 一、审核核心要点

### 1.1 商业模式说明

**采用"喵币"虚拟货币体系**：
- 喵币为消耗型虚拟货币，通过 IAP 购买
- 用户用喵币兑换食物、主题、VIP 服务等
- 喵币不可提现、不可跨平台转移

**VIP 服务说明**：
- VIP 为限时特权服务，非自动续费订阅
- 用户用喵币兑换固定天数（如 30 天、90 天）
- 到期后需重新兑换，用户完全掌控

### 1.2 审核话术（英文）

```
Pink House is a virtual pet nurturing app. Users adopt and care for cute pets by purchasing "Meow Coins" through Apple's In-App Purchase system. These coins are used to buy food, unlock themes, and activate VIP privileges within the app.

Our VIP feature is designed as a flexible, on-demand service. Users purchase Meow Coins via IAP and decide when to exchange them for specific service durations (e.g., 30 days or 90 days) based on their pet's growth stage. Since this is not a recurring monthly commitment but a one-time service activation triggered by the user, the Consumable IAP model provides the most transparent and user-controlled experience.

All virtual items (food, themes, VIP) are consumed within the app and cannot be transferred, refunded, or converted to real currency.
```

## 六、常见拒审原因及对策

### 6.1 "绕过内购"（Guideline 3.1.1）

**风险点**：喵币兑换 VIP 可能被质疑
**对策**：
- 强调 VIP 是"限时特权"而非"订阅"
- 用户主动兑换，非自动续费
- 提供上述审核话术

### 6.2 "恢复购买"缺失（Guideline 3.1.2）

**检查点**：
- VIP 订阅必须有恢复功能 ✅
- 恢复按钮明显可见 ✅

### 6.3 "误导性定价"

**避免**：
- 首充双倍必须在首次购买时生效
- 价格必须清晰标注
- 避免"免费"字样（除非真的免费）

### 6.4 "不完整的 App"

**确保**：
- 所有按钮可点击
- 无占位符内容
- 审核测试账号有充足喵币
