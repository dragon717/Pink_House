## Description

Photograph a dress and keep its size, tags and purchase details together. For preorders, record the deposit and see when the balance is due. Open your outfit diary to look back at what you wore.

Browse the Time Hall to discover dresses and add favorites to your wardrobe. You can buy Meow Coins in the app and exchange them for virtual items or a fixed period of VIP access. VIP ends when that period runs out.
