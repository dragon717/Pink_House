## Description

Pink House is your cozy digital closet and virtual pet companion.

Organize your wardrobe, track outfit costs and deposits, keep an outfit diary, and nurture adorable pets in a charming small world. Customize with theme skins, unlock VIP perks, and enjoy AI-powered styling suggestions.

**Virtual currency:** Purchase Meow Coins via In-App Purchase to unlock themes, pet items, and VIP time. VIP is exchanged manually with Meow Coins—it is **not** an auto-renewing subscription.

**Features:**
- Wardrobe management with photos, tags, and filters
- Wealth tracking for deposits and balances
- Outfit diary and sharing
- Virtual pet care and chat
- Theme skins and seasonal decorations
- iCloud sync for your data

Virtual items cannot be refunded or converted to real money. See our Privacy Policy and Terms for details.
